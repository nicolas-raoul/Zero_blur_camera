Zero Blur camera

Recent high-end smartphone cameras have larger lenses, which results in:
- Good: Better picture quality,
- Bad: Unability to focus on both background and foreground(*).

Zero Blur camera solves this problem, making your full picture sharp.
It works by taking 10 pictures at various depths, and taking the sharp parts from each.

Limitation: You must hold the phone very still for 2 seconds while the pictures are being taken, and avoid moving objects such as people walking in the background.

(*) Shallow depth-of-field can be desirable if you want to shoot "bokeh" pieces of art, but there are times when you need fully sharp pictures.
